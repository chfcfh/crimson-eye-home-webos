CleanYourTV replaces LG's Home on a rooted LG webOS TV with a clean launcher of your own apps, and switches off LG's ads and tracking. Everything it changes is kept in RAM or in its own folders and can be undone from the app.

After installing: open CleanYourTV from the app list and choose Set up now. It takes about a minute and the screen blinks once.

Needs webOS 5 or newer (2020 models and later). Updates installed here are taken into use by the service within a few minutes; the app can also update itself.

Documentation: lasharor.github.io/cleanyourtv-WebOS
